Muse Spark proposes product opportunities from real patient comments, a second pass tries to disprove each one, and Muse Image draws what survives. Every claim traces back to its source.

  1. 1ProposeMuse Spark reads the signals and proposes opportunities.
  2. 2VerifyA second pass checks each claim against only its cited comments.
  3. 3ScoreCode scores evidence, source spread, and verifier confidence.
  4. 4VisualizeMuse Image draws the concept, Muse Spark checks its features.

How the score works. Out of 100: evidence (up to 40, from distinct cited comments), source spread (up to 30, from distinct forum threads), and verifier confidence (up to 30). The model never assigns the score itself.

Data. Short excerpts from public patient forums, collected by hand during the hackathon, with a link to each source thread. Threadline Discovery supports product decisions for brand teams. It does not give medical advice.
