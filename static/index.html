What should adaptive brands build next?

Muse Spark proposes product opportunities from notes on public patient forum posts, an independent open-weight model (Muse Spark Open, on Baseten) tries to disprove each one, and Muse Image draws what survives. Every claim traces back to its source.

  1. 1ProposeMuse Spark reads the signals and proposes opportunities.
  2. 2VerifyMuse Spark Open, an open-weight model on Baseten, checks each claim against only its cited notes.
  3. 3ScoreCode scores evidence, source spread, and verifier confidence.
  4. 4VisualizeMuse Image draws the concept, Muse Spark checks its features.

How the score works. Out of 100: evidence (up to 40, from distinct cited notes), source spread (up to 30, from distinct forum threads), and verifier confidence (up to 30). The model never assigns the score itself.

Data. The notes are written in our own words from public patient forum posts read during the hackathon. No post text is reproduced here, and each note links to its source thread. Threadline Discovery supports product decisions for brand teams. It does not give medical advice.

Models. Muse Spark and Muse Image run through Meta Model API. The verification stage runs Muse Spark Open, an open-weight Muse model, on Baseten partner inference.
